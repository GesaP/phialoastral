# Jewelry Doodles Made Simple: Sketching Fun Rings & Pendants

Turn simple doodles into playful jewelry ideas for rings, charms, and pendants.

## What You'll Learn

This relaxed, beginner-friendly class shows you how simple shapes can become expressive jewelry sketches with shine and dimension.

## Course Highlights

- Turn hearts, stars, swirls, and other doodles into jewelry ideas
- Sketch playful rings, charms, and pendants
- Add shine and dimension with simple drawing techniques
- Understand how jewelers use sketches to develop designs
- Build confidence through a relaxed sketchbook process

## Who This Course Is For

- Beginners curious about jewelry sketching
- Jewelry designers looking for a playful drawing process
- Anyone interested in creative expression
- Experienced artists who want to loosen up their ideas

## Materials Needed

- Paper and pencils
- Your favorite drawing tools
- An open mind and willingness to experiment
